Wicket puts any staging or preview site behind a sign-in. Your client opens a link, signs in with their email, and sees the site. You can take their access away at any time.

Open dashboard $ npx wicket protect https://staging.example.com
01

Point it at a site

Give Wicket the address of a staging or preview site. It returns a new address that asks for a sign-in first.

02

Invite by email

Add the email of each client, with an end time if you want one. They sign in through Kinde. There is no password to share.

03

Take it back

Remove an email and the site closes to that person. A site you mark strict closes the moment you remove them.

How a check stays fast and stays true

The gate runs in Cloudflare's data centers. It checks the sign-in itself and keeps copies of the answer to "may this person open this site", and each copy has a time limit.
